# Task 1 – Donation Campaign Poster

## Project Title
CharityConnect – Donation Campaign Poster

## Description
This poster is designed for a charity donation campaign under the brand name **CharityConnect**.

The main purpose of this poster is to encourage people to donate and support families in need. The design focuses on kindness, community support, education, food, and basic necessities.

## Poster Details

- **Design Type:** Digital Poster / Flyer
- **Campaign:** Donation Campaign
- **Brand:** CharityConnect
- **Size:** A4
- **Dimensions:** 2480 × 3508 px
- **Orientation:** Portrait
- **Format:** PNG

## Main Content

### Headline
**MAKE A DIFFERENCE TODAY**

### Subheadline
**Your Small Donation Can Change a Life**

### Description
Help provide food, education and basic necessities to families in need.

### Call to Action
**DONATE NOW**

## Color Palette

The poster uses a warm and simple charity-themed color palette:

- Cream: `#FFF7ED`
- Dark Brown: `#5A3525`
- Orange: `#C87941`
- Dark Text: `#29221F`

## Typography

- **Headings:** Montserrat ExtraBold
- **Body Text:** Poppins
- **CTA:** Bold Sans Serif

## Design Elements

The poster includes:

- CharityConnect branding
- Large and bold headline
- Donation-related visual
- Food, education and community support icons
- Clear call-to-action button
- Decorative brown and cream shapes
- Simple and readable layout

## Objective

The objective of this poster is to create awareness about charitable giving and motivate people to contribute towards helping families in need.

## Tools Used

- Canva
- PNG Export
